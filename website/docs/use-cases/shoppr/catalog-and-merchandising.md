---
title: Catalog And Merchandising
---

Davenda expects catalog and merchandising to be product concerns, not just a database table and a template loop.

Shoppr demonstrates:

- collection-first browsing
- campaign-led home and collection pages
- product detail as the decision point
- market-aware assortment visibility
- route-level locale handling

## What To Study In Shoppr

- `app.toml` for site and locale setup
- `catalog.toml` for site-scoped collection and product visibility
- `templates/pages/home.html` for the editorial homepage shell
- `templates/commerce/collection-detail.html` for collection storytelling
- `templates/commerce/product-detail.html` for product-page interaction layering

## Product Guidance

If inventory, price, or business rules differ, use sites.

If only language and formatting differ, use locales.

That keeps the commercial model honest.
