# Coil website direction

The Fission static site follows an open editorial system: hierarchy comes from
type, whitespace, alignment, and long rules rather than nested containers.
Visible boxes are reserved for real controls or trust boundaries.

The generated design reference is
[`assets/design/coil-home-direction.png`](assets/design/coil-home-direction.png).

The reference was generated with the built-in image generation tool using this
prompt set:

> Create a high-fidelity desktop website design mockup for Coil, a serious Rust
> product framework rebuilt on Fission. Use a warm off-white paper background,
> near-black ink, one restrained rust-red accent and a small amount of cobalt
> for links. Compose the page as an open editorial broadsheet: a small wordmark
> and sparse navigation above a thin rule; a very large asymmetric headline,
> “Build the product. Keep the platform coherent.”; a concise supporting
> paragraph; then a horizontal execution sequence labelled SSR, ISLANDS, WEB,
> OPERATIONS separated by hairline dividers. Follow with two generous columns
> explaining public product surfaces and operational surfaces, and finish with
> one direct call to action. Hierarchy must come from typography, whitespace,
> alignment and long rules. Show enough of the page to establish the complete
> system. No nested rounded cards, no card wall, no glassmorphism, no gradients,
> no ornamental shadows, no dashboard metrics, no decorative blobs, no fake
> browser chrome, and no generic developer-tool landing-page tropes. Crisp,
> calm, accessible, production-quality editorial art direction.
