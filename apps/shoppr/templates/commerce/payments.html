Operator guidance

Pending payment is still live

A pending payment means the checkout handoff completed but the signed provider webhook has not settled local state yet. Do not treat that as an immediate checkout failure.

Captured means downstream work starts

Once the provider callback reconciles, use the next-step lane to confirm order confirmation, fulfillment, or customer follow-up work is appropriate.

Refunds stay coupled to the order

Move into order detail for refund issuance or fulfillment. This page is for provider state, webhook timing, and follow-up visibility, not deep order mutation.
