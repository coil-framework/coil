Gitly demonstrates that Davenda’s customer-app model survives intact outside ecommerce.

## The Main Product Shape

Gitly owns repository-style pages, organization pages, search, actions, and profile surfaces.

Those are expressed through customer-owned routes and templates rather than by forcing a commerce-oriented vocabulary onto the app.

## The Important Templates

Gitly’s main product templates include:

- `templates/gitly/home.html`
- `templates/gitly/repository.html`
- `templates/gitly/issues.html`
- `templates/gitly/pulls.html`
- `templates/gitly/actions.html`
- `templates/gitly/organization.html`
- `templates/gitly/profile.html`
- `templates/gitly/search.html`

Together they show a non-store information architecture implemented through the same runtime principles as Shoppr.
